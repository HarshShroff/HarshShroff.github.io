Privacy Policy

For the "Harsh Shroff" Google OAuth app (a personal assistant). Last updated October 8, 2026.

Who this app is for

This app is a personal tool built and used only by Harsh Shroff, the owner of this website, to manage his own Google account. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's account is connected to it.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app can access the following data from his own account, only as needed to run his personal assistant:

  • Gmail: reading messages and labels, creating drafts, applying labels, archiving, and sending email that the owner has approved.
  • Google Calendar: reading events, creating events, and updating or responding to events the owner has approved.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the owner himself: summarizing his inbox and calendar, drafting replies, organizing mail, and scheduling. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

Where the data is stored

Data retrieved from Google is processed on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never published. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Text may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses on his own behalf, under that provider's terms, solely to carry out his requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local copies are kept only as long as needed for the owner's own records. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the app can no longer access the account, and locally stored tokens are deleted.
