About Me

I'm an AI/ML Engineer and Data Scientist passionate about building intelligent systems that solve real-world problems.

My journey in AI and data science began with a fascination for how intelligent systems could solve real-world problems. With a background in Electronics and Communications Engineering from Vishwakarma Government Engineering College, I developed a strong foundation in technical problem-solving before pursuing my Master's in Data Science at UMBC.

Working with robotics at UMBC's AI and Robotics Center opened my eyes to the possibilities of combining physical systems with artificial intelligence. My experience at The Conservation Fund showed me how AI could be applied to environmental and sustainability challenges.

Now at VITG Corp., I'm focused on creating AI solutions that automate processes and extract insights from complex data sources, with a particular interest in generative AI and its applications across industries.
